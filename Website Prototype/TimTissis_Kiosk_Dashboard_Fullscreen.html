💧 Water Refill Threshold

💧

Auto-Refill Trigger Level Solenoid valve opens when water drops below this threshold

When the water level sensor (JSN-SR04T) detects the trough level has fallen below the configured threshold, the system automatically opens the solenoid valve to refill until the trough reaches 100% (5 L maximum capacity). The threshold is configurable per pen by the caretaker.

0% (0 L)25% (1.25 L)50% (2.5 L)75% (3.75 L)100% (5 L)
%
= 2.00 L of the 5 L trough capacity

⚠ Trough max capacity: 5 L. Recommended threshold range: 30% – 50% (1.5 L – 2.5 L). A threshold below 20% may allow prolonged water deprivation. A threshold above 60% may cause the valve to cycle excessively. The caretaker or farm owner should validate this value with manual depth measurements before deployment.
