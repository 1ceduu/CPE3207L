\section{Goals and Objectives}

The main goal of this project is to design and develop an integrated automated swine feeding and watering system with centralized data monitoring and control.

To achieve this goal, the system must fulfill the following specific objectives:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Implement a programmable central control unit capable of real-time monitoring and control of feed storage, scheduled feed dispensing, water level monitoring, and automatic water dispensing when the level falls below the required threshold.
  \item Design individual feeder modules capable of monitoring feed consumption, water level, and ammonia levels, with defined validation baselines for feeding, water availability, and air-quality thresholds.
  \item Develop a centralized touchscreen user interface for system monitoring, data acquisition, historical logging, and graphical visualization of events relevant to final project checking.
  \item Integrate and validate a multi-channel alert system that notifies caretakers of consumption and environmental anomalies through the local dashboard and SMS notifications via a LoRa-GSM gateway, and evaluate its improvement over manual monitoring through controlled comparison, owner user-acceptance testing (UAT), Likert-scale confidence ratings, response-time measurements, and caretaker feedback.
\end{itemize}
