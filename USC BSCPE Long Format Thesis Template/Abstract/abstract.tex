\begin{abstract}
Manual feeding and inadequate health monitoring in commercial piggeries contribute to feed waste, undetected illness, and preventable livestock losses. This thesis presents the design and development of an integrated, hardwired automated feeding and watering system with centralized data monitoring for commercial piggeries. The system employs a main control module connected to multiple feeder modules via a wired RS-485/MODBUS RTU network, integrating load cells, ultrasonic water level sensors, water flow sensors, and ammonia air quality sensors to ensure precise feed rationing and continuous environmental monitoring. Collected telemetry is processed and displayed through a centralized touchscreen user interface running on a Raspberry Pi, enabling farm caretakers to track consumption patterns, observe pen-level environmental trends, and detect early signs of illness in near real time. An automated notification pipeline incorporates both on-dashboard alerts and remote SMS alerts dispatched via a LoRa-to-GSM gateway. By eliminating reliance on subjective manual observation and vulnerable wireless infrastructure in harsh agricultural settings, the system offers a reliable, robust, and scalable engineering solution for improving biosecurity, animal welfare, and operational efficiency in swine production.
\end{abstract}
